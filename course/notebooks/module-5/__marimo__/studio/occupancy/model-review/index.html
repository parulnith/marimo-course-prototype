How the threshold trades one error for the other

Each point is the same evaluation run at another slider position. The dashed line marks the current threshold.

False positive

The room was empty, but the sensors said it was occupied. The lights or air conditioning may stay on in an empty room, wasting energy.

Raising the threshold usually means fewer of these.

False negative

People were in the room, but the sensors said it was empty. The lights could switch off while someone is still there.

Lowering the threshold usually means fewer of these.
